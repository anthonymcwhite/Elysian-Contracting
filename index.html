A smarter home, made simpler

Don't let your home be smarter than you.

From thermostats and doorbells to televisions and appliances, more of our homes are connected than ever. You shouldn't need to be a technology expert to feel comfortable using them.

Ask about a home network health check: a practical review of connected devices, Wi-Fi settings, common security risks, and ways to improve privacy and control.

What can a home network health check cover?

  • Understanding what's connected to your network
  • Reviewing router and Wi-Fi security settings
  • Checking guest access and device separation
  • Identifying common privacy and security concerns
  • Explaining findings in plain language

Assessment scope is agreed upon in advance; intrusive testing is not included in a standard health check.
